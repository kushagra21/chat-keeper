How to export a chat from WhatsApp

iPhone

  1. Open the chat and tap the name at the top.
  2. Scroll down and tap Export Chat.
  3. Choose Attach Media to keep photos and videos.
  4. Save the zip to Files, or AirDrop it to your computer.

Android

  1. Open the chat and tap ⋮ › More › Export chat.
  2. Choose Include media.
  3. Save it to Drive or Files, or send it to your computer.

WhatsApp limits how many messages and how much media one export can hold. Very long chats may come out shortened; that happens in WhatsApp, before Chat Keeper sees the file.

What you get

  • A readable chat with photos, videos, voice notes and documents in place.
  • Search across every message, filter by person, jump to any date.
  • An offline archive: a zip with a page that opens in any browser, every file byte for byte, and the original text.
  • A PDF of the whole chat or a date range, with photos inline and page numbers.
  • SHA-256 checksums of the original export and every file, for records and disputes.
